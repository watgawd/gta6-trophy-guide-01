Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions alone: story first, strangers while they are open, collectibles as you travel, and the long grinds last. The platinum pops when the other 54 are done — there is no extra step.

100% completion (A Long Road, gold) is the spine of the whole run: story, stranger strands, collectibles, activities and challenges all feed it. When it pops, the platinum is usually only a handful of clean-up trophies away.
